# Dark Liquid Glass — schedule surface

Mode: Operate. Code-led implementation. The user pinned the existing HTML reference `ai_studio_code.html`; no concept roll or generated comp is needed for this precisely specified redesign. Real schedules, source warnings and existing navigation remain product truth.

## World
True black ground, static neutral ambient wash, glossy translucent 22px cards with a bright upper bevel. Inter text and JetBrains Mono time/room numerals are hosted locally. Practice is violet, laboratory amber, lecture rose; current overrides type with emerald and past becomes neutral. Mixed parallel pairs keep separate type labels. Glass is explicitly requested by the reference.

## First viewport
Compact group/date/search/settings header, week selector, six day pills with actual lesson-type dots and accessible counts, one truthful source-status chip, then chronological cards. Mobile overview follows the agenda; desktop keeps it in the left sidebar. Sparse or unavailable sources stay explicit.

## Visitor path
Student opens saved group, checks the nearest room/time, scans the day, optionally opens lesson details. Search and rooms are the three-item navigation; other tools remain in More.

## Signature interaction
Occasional pointer-opened sheets enter in 200ms (opacity + 12px translation, scale .985); keyboard opens instantly and reduced motion disables travel. Day selection stays immediate, with horizontal swipes on the calendar strip. Mobile sheets dismiss by dragging their handle; focus returns to the opener. Glass is static, not a moving background.

## Cross-surface reach and honest risk
Desktop uses overview + agenda; <=700px puts the agenda first and overview below it. Search, rooms, note, menu and other dialogs share glossy glass. Light/system, reduced motion and reduced transparency remain. Opaque blur fallbacks and 12px functional minimum support readable controls. Actual installed-phone behavior is not measured here.
