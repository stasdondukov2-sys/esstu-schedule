Mode: Operate. Code-led implementation. The user confirmed student day and nearest lesson as priority, then explicitly pinned dark plus glass. Direction seed: user-pinned; no random seed or raster comp. The Impeccable context launcher failed with access denied; context was read directly. ## World
Graphite ground, subdued lavender, real translucent panels, familiar app typography. No marketing imagery or claims. Glass is explicitly requested, not an accidental default.

## First viewport
Group selector, day/date, nearest/current lesson and large room; then week and calendar strip. Sparse or unavailable sources must remain honest rather than inventing a live lesson.

## Visitor path
Student opens saved group, checks the nearest room/time, scans the day, optionally opens lesson details. Search and rooms are the three-item navigation; other tools remain in More.

## Signature interaction
Occasional pointer-opened sheets enter in 200ms (opacity + 12px translation, scale .985); keyboard opens instantly and reduced motion disables travel. Day selection stays immediate, with horizontal swipes on the calendar strip. Mobile sheets dismiss by dragging their handle; focus returns to the opener. Glass is static, not a moving background.

## Cross-surface reach and honest risk
Desktop uses overview + agenda, <=700px stacks. Search, rooms, note, menu and other dialogs share glass. Light/system remain. Strong transparency can reduce contrast: the built panels use .82/.84 alpha and opaque fallbacks. Actual installed-phone behavior is not measured here.
